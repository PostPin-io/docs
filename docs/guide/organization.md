# Organization

## Members

**Members** in the header lists everyone in the organization with their role.

<Screenshot name="members" alt="The members page of an organization" />

- Change a member's role with the role selector, if your own role allows it.
- **Access** shows which projects and timelines a member or reviewer can see, and removes access.
- **Remove** removes someone from the organization. They lose access to everything in it.
- **Invite by E-Mail** sends an invitation to the whole organization. Pending invitations are listed
  with **Resend** and **Revoke**.
- **Invite links** has one join link per role. Anyone with an enabled link can join with that
  role. **Reset** a link to invalidate the old one.

Only admins and owners can invite to the whole organization. Members invite people to single
projects or timelines from the [share dialog](./sharing.md#share-a-project-or-timeline).

## Settings

Admins and owners see **Settings** in the header.

<Screenshot name="organization-settings" alt="The organization settings" />

Owners can rename the organization here. The **Danger zone** has **Delete organization**: type the
organization's name to confirm, then click **Delete forever**. This deletes all projects,
timelines, exports, markers and videos of the organization and can't be undone.

## Several organizations

You can create more organizations from the dashboard or your [profile](./account.md#organizations),
and be invited to others. The account menu at the top right lists them under **Organizations**.
Click one to switch to it.
