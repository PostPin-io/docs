# Getting started

You need a PostPin account and an organization. Everything in PostPin (projects, timelines,
markers) belongs to an organization.

## Create an account

Open your PostPin server in the browser and click **Create an account** on the login page. Enter
your first and last name, your email address and a password, then click **Sign up**. You are signed
in right away. If the server offers it, you can also sign up with **Continue with Google**, **Sign
in with Microsoft**, GitHub, Apple or your company's single sign-on instead. If the server's
operator has set up terms of service, a data processing agreement or a privacy policy, the sign-up
page links them, and the footer links the imprint and privacy policy.

<Screenshot name="signup" alt="The sign-up page" />

If someone invited you, use the link in the invitation email instead. It takes you to the
organization you were invited to, and you sign up or log in from there. See
[Invitations](./sharing.md#accept-an-invitation).

:::tip

You can verify your email address later from your [profile](./account.md#email). PostPin works
without it. Only the [system admin area](../self-hosting/admin.md) of a self-hosted server requires
a verified address.

:::

## Log in

<Screenshot name="login" alt="The login page" />

Log in with your email and password, with a provider button, or with **Sign in with a passkey** if
you have [added a passkey](./account.md#passkeys). If you turned on
[two-factor authentication](./account.md#two-factor-authentication), PostPin asks for the code from
your authenticator app next.

## Your first upload

After signing up, PostPin opens the dashboard and starts the **Your first upload** tutorial. It
covers the whole window and builds what you need for your first review:

1. **Welcome to PostPin**: click **Let's go**.
2. **Create your organization**: your company or team. You become its owner.
3. **Create your first project**: one production, for example a film or a commercial.
4. **Add a timeline**: the sequence you are editing. Every version you upload of it becomes an
   export.
5. **Upload your first export**: **Choose a video** opens the upload dialog. PostPin reads the
   file's frame rate and start timecode.

<Screenshot name="first-upload" alt="The Your first upload tutorial at the step that creates the organization" />

Each form suggests a name, so **Continue** alone is enough. While the export uploads and
processes, **Open your export** takes you to it. It plays as soon as it's ready.

On the export, the **Your first review** tutorial (not on a phone) shows how to leave a marker, give it a category
and reply to it. After that, the **PostPin Guides** tour shows where all
[guides](./guides.md) are.

**Skip tutorial** ends it at any step. A reload goes on where you were. To start it again, use the
[PostPin Guides](./guides.md) page in the account menu. While you aren't a member of any
organization, the dashboard shows a welcome card. Its **Get started** button starts the tutorial
again, also when guides are switched off. Once you completed or skipped the tutorial, the card
offers **Create organization** instead.

If you are already in an organization, for example because you were invited as a member, the
tutorial leaves out the organization step, also in its step bar. Invited reviewers can't create projects, so they don't
get it.

## Organizations

You can be a member of several organizations. Switch between them in the account menu at the top
right, under **Organizations**. On a phone, the account menu is the **☰** button at the top left.
