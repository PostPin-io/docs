# Getting started

You need a PostPin account and an organization. Everything in PostPin (projects, timelines,
markers) belongs to an organization.

## Create an account

Open your PostPin server in the browser and click **Create an account** on the login page. Enter
your first and last name, your email address and a password, then click **Sign Up**. You are signed
in right away. If the server's operator has set up terms of service, a data processing agreement or
a privacy policy, the sign-up page links them, and the footer links the imprint and privacy policy.

<Screenshot name="signup" alt="The sign-up page" />

If someone invited you, use the link in the invitation email instead. It takes you to the
organization you were invited to, and you sign up or log in from there. See
[Invitations](./sharing.md#accept-an-invitation).

:::tip

You can verify your email address later from your [profile](./account.md#email). PostPin works
without it. Only the [system admin area](../self-hosting/admin.md) of a self-hosted server requires
a verified address.

:::

## The tutorial

After signing up, PostPin starts a short tutorial on the **Welcome to PostPin** page. Its steps
build what you need for your first review:

1. **Create your organization**: your company or team. You become its owner.
2. **Create your first project**: one production, for example a film or a commercial.
3. **Add a timeline**: the sequence you are editing. Every version you upload of it becomes an
   export.
4. **Upload your first export**: get the plugin for your editing software, upload from it, and
   wait until the export is ready. **Upload a video file manually** uploads in the browser instead.

<Screenshot name="welcome" alt="The first step of the tutorial: creating the organization" />

On the first export, a few cards explain markers, [pointers](./review.md#pointers) and the
[marker sync](../plugins/index.md#syncing-markers) of the plugins. **Share Guide** next to the names
at the top explains share links and invite links.

**Skip tutorial** ends it at any step. While it isn't finished, the dashboard shows a card to
continue it. Your [profile](./account.md#tutorial) can show it again.

People you invite to your organization don't get the tutorial.

## Organizations

You can be a member of several organizations. Switch between them in the account menu at the top
right, under **Organizations**. On a phone, the account menu is the **☰** button at the top left.

## Next steps

1. [Install the plugin](../plugins/index.md) for your editing software and log in.
2. Render and upload your first timeline to the project and timeline you created, or create new
   ones from the plugin.
3. [Invite your team and clients](./sharing.md) and [review the export](./review.md).
